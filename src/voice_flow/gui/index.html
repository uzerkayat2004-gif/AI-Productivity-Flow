FEATURE 1 · AUDIO FLOW

🔊 Audio Flow: Instant Text to Speech & Summaries

Welcome to Audio Flow, our first feature. Imagine scrolling through Twitter or reading a long document that you don't want to read. Simply select the text, and a small Audio Flow button appears. Click it to convert the article into a spoken audio summary.

"Long Twitter thread or article selected..." 🔊

Pause anytime to read at your own pace or adjust narration speed.

FEATURE 2 · VIDEO FLOW

🎬 Video Flow: Visual Summaries & Explainers

Next is Video Flow, our second feature. If you want to convert that article into a visual summary, Video Flow transforms your document into an animated visual explainer right on your screen.

▶ Article → Visual Explanation Motion Graphic

Switch seamlessly from audio to visual understanding in one click.

FEATURE 3 · VOICE FLOW

🗣️ Voice Flow: Speak & Post Your Thoughts

Now that you have all the context about the article, you can use the Voice Flow feature to post about the article by speaking, and it will write it down for you. Zoom in on your context and let your voice do the writing.

🖱️ Hold Middle Click → 💬 Speak Your Thoughts → ⌨️ Release — Text Appears

AI polishing automatically cleans grammar and spoken punctuation as you talk.
